A place to keep the things worth practising, plus a few free sites that explain
them well. Pick one subject, study for a short block, then test yourself without
looking.

## How to study

- **Recall first.** Close the notes and write down what you remember. Checking
  your answer afterwards is what makes it stick.
- **Space it out.** Revisit a topic after a day, then after a week, instead of
  doing it all in one sitting.
- **Short blocks.** Work for 25 minutes, take a 5 minute break, then start the
  next block.
- **Mix the problems.** Do a few different kinds of problems in one session so
  you have to decide which method to use.
- **Sleep on it.** Sleep is when the practice turns into memory.

## Biology

### Chapter 4: Energy — ATP, Photosynthesis, and Cellular Respiration

This week covers aerobic and anaerobic cell respiration, reviews photosynthesis
and cellular respiration, and finishes with the Chapter 4 test.

#### Day 1 — Monday

**Objective:** To discuss aerobic respiration.

- Notes and discussion: ATP, photosynthesis, and cellular respiration
- Notes and discussion: the Krebs cycle and the electron transport chain (ETC)
- Watch the Amoeba Sisters Cellular Respiration video — due Tuesday at the
  beginning of class

#### Day 2 — Tuesday

**Objective:** To discuss the process of anaerobic cell respiration.

- Finish the POGIL

#### Day 3 — Wednesday

**Objective:** To review the process of photosynthesis and cellular respiration.

- Make review drawings: cellular respiration and photosynthesis
- Group quiz: cellular respiration

#### Day 4 — Thursday

**Objective:** To review for the Chapter 4 test on energy.

- Go over the answers to the Review Packet for Chapter 4
- Review for the test
- Complete the study guide for Thursday

## Before a test

1. Make a one page summary from memory, then fill the gaps from your notes.
2. Answer the review questions out loud, without the book open.
3. Practise the problems you got wrong last time.
4. Pack what you need and go to bed on time.
