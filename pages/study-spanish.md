## Topics to review

- Greetings and introductions: hola, buenos días, me llamo…
- Numbers, dates, and telling time
- Family and school vocabulary
- Present tense of regular -ar, -er, and -ir verbs
- Ser and estar
- Question words: qué, quién, dónde, cuándo, cómo

## Practice

- [Duolingo](https://www.duolingo.com/) — short daily lessons that mix listening,
  reading, and speaking.
- [SpanishDict](https://www.spanishdict.com/) — dictionary, conjugation charts,
  and practice quizzes.
- [Conjuguemos](https://conjuguemos.com/) — timed verb conjugation drills.

## Before a quiz

1. Cover the Spanish column and write the words from the English side.
2. Say each word out loud after you write it.
3. Conjugate one regular -ar, one -er, and one -ir verb from memory.
4. Check the answers, then rewrite only the ones you missed.
